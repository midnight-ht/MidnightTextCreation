# LinkedIn Operator Note

适用平台：linkedin, medium, substack

语气：professional, human, concrete, tradeoff-aware.

句长：short to medium paragraphs for scanning.

开头方式：start with a decision, mistake, tradeoff, or observation from work.

禁止写法：
- thrilled to announce
- game-changing
- unlock your potential
- corporate filler
- inflated achievement language

示例段落：
The fastest way to make AI writing sound more human is not to add warmth. It is to add accountability. A human draft makes choices. It leaves out reasonable alternatives and explains why.

人审标准：
- the point of view is visible
- the example sounds lived-in
- the ending is an observation, not a motivational slogan
